# Changelog

All notable changes to `packstub/filament-gantt` are documented in this file.

## 1.0.0 - Unreleased

First release: a Gantt chart and resource timeline for Filament 4 (4.13+) and 5.

- `Gantt` definition: `query()`, `start()`/`end()` (date or datetime casts, plain strings too), `label()`, `color()`, `progress()`, `milestone()`, `bar()` for the full shape, `groupBy()` by a column or a `BelongsTo` relation with `groups()` for a fixed, complete list (`Group::fromModels()`), `compact()` for the resource-timeline layout, `sortBy()`, `filters()`. `Bar::url()` keeps only `http(s)`, `mailto`, `tel` and relative URLs.
- Pages and widget: `GanttResourcePage` (access follows the resource policy), `GanttPage`, `GanttWidget` (read-only by default), and the `InteractsWithGantt` trait for any Livewire component.
- Axis: day, week and month scales with a two-row header, today line, weekend shading, `firstDayOfWeek()`, `startDate()`/`endDate()` or a padded window around the bars (clamped by `min_window_days`/`max_window_days`), a date-range picker and select/date-range filters (native controls, styled like Filament's); zoom, window and filters kept in the URL, folded groups in local storage. Only the bars overlapping the window are loaded, with plain range predicates so the date columns' indexes stay in use.
- Moving and resizing: pointer-event drag to move (keeping the duration) or to resize either edge, snapped to `snapTo(Unit::Day|Unit::Hour)` relative to where the bar was (the time of day is kept, so datetime records move by whole days with the default unit); the browser stops a resize at `minDuration()`/`maxDuration()`; Escape or a lost pointer puts the bar back; a drag attempt on a bar that cannot move is never a click. Optimistic update, then a renderless `ganttMove`; the server re-checks `readOnly()`, `movable()`, `resizable()` (a change of both edges and the length needs both), `minDuration()`, `maxDuration()`, `lockedBefore()` and `canMove()`, saves (or runs `moveUsing()`), fires `Packstub\Gantt\Events\BarMoved`, runs `afterMove()` and answers with the authoritative bar. A refused move snaps back with a notification. All dates are wall-clock moments tagged UTC, so a daylight-saving change never lengthens or shortens a bar; a record `milestone()` flags keeps its real end, shifted with the start.
- Actions: `barAction()` on click, `barActions()` in a menu on right-click, long-press (touch) or Shift+F10 with arrow-key navigation, `createAction()` on an empty spot with the start, end and group filled in for the fields the form does not have; the group is accepted only when the chart knows it (`canCreateIn()`, `creatableIn()` to decide yourself); records resolved through the chart's query.
- Refresh: `refreshOn([...])` Livewire events, `poll()`, and the `packstub-gantt-refresh` event.
- Hand-written `gantt.js` (one pure `layout()` function, row windowing above 200 rows, bars as `role="button"` with an accessible name) and `gantt.css` on Filament's palette: dark mode, RTL (logical properties, zoom and hint centred either way), no build step, no third-party Gantt library; `composer test:js` runs the browser side on plain Node.
- Translations: en, ro, ru, de.
